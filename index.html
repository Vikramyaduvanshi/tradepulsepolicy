TradePulse – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026  |  Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tradepulse ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use the TradePulse mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide

  • Email address – if you create an account or log in with your email.
  • Search terms – words you type to search for news inside the App.
  • Language preference – the language you choose or type for AI analysis of a news item.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as device type, operating system version, app version, IP address, and crash or error logs, used to keep the App working and secure.
  • Usage information such as which screens or news items you open and how often you use features like AI analysis, used to apply fair-use limits and improve the App.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or payment card details.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App, including showing market and company news.
  • To authenticate you and manage your account.
  • To generate AI-based summaries or analysis of news in the language you request.
  • To prevent abuse and enforce usage limits (for example, a daily limit on AI analysis).
  • To diagnose problems, fix bugs, and improve performance and features.
  • To respond to your queries and support requests.

3. AI Analysis and Third-Party Services

When you use the AI analysis feature, the text of the selected news item (its headline and details) and your chosen language are sent from our server to a third-party AI service provider to generate the analysis. We do not intentionally send your email address or other personal information to the AI provider as part of this request. The AI provider processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may also use other service providers for hosting, databases, authentication, and analytics. These providers process data on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • with service providers that help us run the App (hosting, database, AI processing), as described above;
  • if required by law, regulation, legal process, or a government request;
  • to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the App, or the public.

5. Data Security

Data between the App and our servers is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will process verified requests within a reasonable period.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Not Financial Advice

TradePulse provides news and AI-generated summaries for informational and educational purposes only. Nothing in the App is investment, financial, legal, or tax advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. AI-generated content can be incomplete or inaccurate. Always do your own research and consult a qualified financial adviser before making investment decisions. We are not responsible for any losses arising from the use of information in the App.

10. Third-Party Links

The App may display content that contains links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
